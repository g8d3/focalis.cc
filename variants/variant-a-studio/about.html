ABOUT — FOCALIS S.A.S.

A small studio,
built to last.

Focalis Studio is a focused software studio based in Bogotá, Colombia. We design and ship minimal, premium digital products for creators, operators, and teams who automate their work — without the bloat.

What we build

We build primitives — small, composable tools that do one thing exceptionally. Flow Engine orchestrates workflows. Capture Kit captures reality. Sync Layer keeps truth in one place. Frame Foundry turns code into video. Each can stand alone; together they form a studio stack.

Our products are digital goods: downloadable software, templates, and licensed services. No physical shipments. Instant delivery via Dodo Payments as merchant of record — they handle checkout, taxes, and invoices so we can focus on the product.
