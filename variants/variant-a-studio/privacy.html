LEGAL — PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective: January 1, 2026 · Last updated: January 1, 2026 · Focalis S.A.S., Bogotá, Colombia

In short: we collect the minimum needed to deliver your purchases and answer your messages. We don’t sell your data. Payment is handled by Dodo Payments as merchant of record — they handle card data, we never see it. Our products run locally where possible and don’t phone home with your content.

1. Who we are

Focalis S.A.S. (NIT 900.XXX.XXX-1) is a simplified stock company incorporated in Bogotá, D.C., Colombia. We operate the website focalis.cc (“Site”) and sell digital products — downloadable software, templates, and licensed services (collectively, “Products”).

Contact for privacy matters: privacy@focalis.cc and hello@focalis.cc. Correspondence address: Bogotá, D.C., Colombia (full address appears on your Dodo Payments invoice).

2. What we collect

2.1 You provide directly

  • Contact & support: name, email, message content when you use our contact form or email us.
  • Purchase information: we receive from Dodo Payments: your email, name (if provided), product purchased, amount, and license key reference. We do not receive or store your card number, CVV, or full payment credentials — those are processed by Dodo Payments.
  • License usage (optional): when you activate a product, we may record a hashed license key, product version, and platform (e.g., “macOS 14”) to prevent abuse. No file content is transmitted unless you explicitly use a cloud feature (e.g., Sync Layer webhooks).

2.2 Automatically

  • Log data: IP address, browser, and pages visited, processed for security and aggregated analytics. We use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics (no cross-site tracking). You can block cookies and the site still works.
  • Cookies: only essential cookies for the site to function (e.g., remembering that you dismissed a banner). No advertising cookies. See §6.

2.3 We do not collect

We don’t intentionally collect sensitive categories (health, biometrics, precise location) and we don’t build advertising profiles. Our downloadable products (Flow Engine, Capture Kit, Frame Foundry, Signal Desk) run locally by default; your workflow files and captures stay on your machine unless you choose to sync or export them.

3. Why we process data (legal bases)

We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis:

  • Contract — to deliver the Product, license, and support you purchased.
  • Legitimate interest — to secure the Site, prevent fraud/abuse, and improve the Products (using aggregated, non-identifying data).
  • Consent — when you submit a contact form or opt into updates (you can withdraw anytime).
  • Legal obligation — to comply with Colombian law and with tax rules via our merchant of record.

4. Merchant of record — Dodo Payments

Checkout, payment processing, VAT/GST calculation, and invoicing are handled by Dodo Payments as merchant of record. When you click “Buy with Dodo,” you are redirected to or embedded in Dodo’s checkout. Dodo acts as an independent controller for payment data. Their privacy policy and terms apply to the payment step. We receive only the confirmation data needed to fulfill your order (email, product, transaction ID).

If you need a VAT invoice, receipt, or to exercise rights over payment data, you can contact Dodo via the details in your receipt and also reach us at support@focalis.cc — we’ll coordinate.

5. How we use data

  • Deliver and license Products, send download links and keys, and provide support.
  • Respond to contact and refund requests.
  • Send product updates or security notices related to a Product you own (you can opt out of non-essential updates).
  • Maintain security, prevent license abuse, and generate aggregated, anonymized usage statistics.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. Cookies

This Site uses only essential cookies (if any): one to remember cookie preferences and, when you use the contact form, a CSRF token. No analytics or ad cookies are set without your action. You can block or delete cookies in your browser; the Site remains functional.

If we add analytics in the future (e.g., privacy-first, aggregated), we will update this section and, where required, ask for consent.

7. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Dodo Payments (merchant of record) — to process purchases, taxes, and invoices.
  • Service providers — email delivery (e.g., transactional email) and hosting, bound by confidentiality and only as needed to run the Site.
  • Legal — when required by Colombian law or to protect our rights, after assessing the request.

No sale of data. No sharing for third-party marketing.

8. International transfers

We are based in Colombia. If you access the Site from outside Colombia, your contact messages and purchase confirmations may be processed in Colombia and in the locations where Dodo Payments and our hosting/email providers operate. We rely on contractual safeguards where required.

9. Retention

  • Purchase & license records: kept for 5 years for tax, accounting, and license-verification purposes (Colombian commercial obligations).
  • Support messages: kept for 2 years after last interaction, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Logs: aggregated or deleted within 90 days.

10. Security

We use TLS for the Site, least-privilege access, and we minimize what we store. No system is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable technical and organizational measures and we will notify you and authorities where legally required if a breach affects your data.

Our local-first products are designed so your content doesn’t need to leave your machine. Sync Layer is end-to-end encrypted where advertised — you hold the keys; we cannot read your synced content.

11. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including Colombia’s Law 1581 / Habeas Data, and where applicable GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object, restrict, or port your data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise them, email privacy@focalis.cc with subject “Privacy Request.” We respond within 15 business days (often sooner). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (Colombia) or your local authority.

For payment data held by Dodo Payments, you can also contact Dodo directly via your receipt; we will assist if you copy us.

12. Children

The Site and Products are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

We will post changes here and update the “Last updated” date. For material changes we will also notify customers by email where we have it. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

14. Contact

Focalis S.A.S., Bogotá, D.C., Colombia — privacy@focalis.cc · hello@focalis.cc. For support with orders, use support@focalis.cc or the contact form.

This policy is provided for transparency and payment-processor review. It is not legal advice. For specific compliance, consult counsel in your jurisdiction. Spanish translation available upon request — English is the controlling version for this Site.
