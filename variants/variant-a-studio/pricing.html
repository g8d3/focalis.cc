Frequently asked

What does “one-time” mean? ⌄

You pay once and own the version you bought forever. You get one year of priority updates and support. After that, you keep everything — optional renewal is available at a discount if you want continued updates, but it’s never required.

How is delivery handled? ⌄

Checkout is via Dodo Payments (our merchant of record). Immediately after payment you receive an email with your license key and download link. If you don’t see it within 5 minutes, check spam or write to hello@focalis.cc.

Can I use this commercially? ⌄

Yes. Every license includes commercial use. You can use products for client work, team workflows, and revenue-generating projects. Redistribution or resale of the product itself is not permitted (see Terms).

Do you offer refunds? ⌄

Yes — 14 calendar days, no questions asked, for any product you haven’t heavily abused. See our Refund Policy for details. Request via hello@focalis.cc.

What about taxes and invoices? ⌄

Dodo Payments handles VAT/GST and issues the invoice/receipt as merchant of record. Your invoice is emailed after purchase and available in your Dodo customer portal.

Questions? Contact us — we reply within 24 hours (Bogotá, UTC-5).
