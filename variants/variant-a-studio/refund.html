LEGAL — REFUND POLICY

Refund Policy

Effective: January 1, 2026 · Applies to all digital products sold via Dodo Payments (merchant of record)

✓

14-day, no-questions-asked refunds.

If a Focalis product isn’t right for you, email us within 14 calendar days of purchase and we’ll refund you — no forms, no pushback. Refunds are issued via Dodo Payments to your original payment method.

1. Overview

Focalis S.A.S. (“Focalis,” “we”) sells digital products — downloadable software, templates, and licensed services. Delivery is electronic and instant after payment via Dodo Payments (merchant of record). Because delivery is immediate, you acknowledge that performance begins at purchase — but we still offer a generous refund window below, going beyond the minimum required by law in many places.

2. Refund window

You may request a full refund for any Product within 14 calendar days from the purchase date (“Refund Window”), no questions asked, subject to the fair-use limits in §3.

Example: purchase on May 1 → refund request must be sent by May 15 (inclusive, Bogotá time UTC-5).

3. Fair-use limits (to prevent abuse)

We keep this simple and human. A refund may be declined or offered as store credit only if there is clear abuse, such as:

  • You have requested 3+ refunds in the last 12 months for the same Product;
  • We detect systematic license abuse (e.g., keys shared publicly, large-scale redistribution);
  • You are past the 14-day window (see §4 for exceptions).

Normal evaluation — installing, testing workflows, rendering a few videos, syncing a folder — is expected and never counts as abuse. We don’t monitor your content; abuse is flagged only by license-server anomalies (e.g., same key activated on many distinct machines).

4. After 14 days

After the Refund Window, refunds are not guaranteed but we will still try to help:

  • Technical issue we can’t fix: if a Product is fundamentally broken on your supported platform and we can’t resolve it within a reasonable time, we will refund even after 14 days.
  • Duplicate purchase: accidental duplicate charge for the same Product — full refund anytime.
  • Charge not recognized: contact us and Dodo; we’ll investigate and refund if it’s a duplicate or error.

5. What’s not refundable

  • Products obtained outside Dodo checkout (e.g., free review copies) — nothing was charged.
  • Fees charged by your bank or card issuer (e.g., foreign-transaction fees) — those are between you and your bank.
  • Bundle refunds are for the entire bundle (§6).

6. Bundles

The Studio Bundle ($554) is a single purchase. Within 14 days you may:

  • Request a full bundle refund (all six products deactivated), or
  • Keep the bundle and request a partial adjustment if you already owned one included Product purchased within the last 30 days — contact us and we’ll credit the difference.

We don’t offer partial bundle refunds for “I only want one product” — instead, buy the single product you need (from $19).

7. How to request a refund

Email support@focalis.cc (copy hello@focalis.cc if you prefer) with:

  • Subject: “Refund request”
  • Purchase email address
  • Order ID / transaction ID from your Dodo receipt (if you have it — we can look it up)
  • Product name (optional: one-line reason, but not required within 14 days)

You can also use the contact form and select “Refund request.” We acknowledge refund requests within 1 business day and process eligible refunds within 2 business days.

8. How refunds are processed

  • Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments to the original payment method (card, etc.). We initiate, Dodo executes.
  • Processing time after we initiate: typically 5–10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank/card issuer.
  • You will receive a confirmation email from Dodo (and from us) when the refund is issued.
  • Your license key for the refunded Product is deactivated after the refund is issued. You should delete local copies.

9. Chargebacks

If you paid via card and don’t recognize a charge, please contact us first at support@focalis.cc — we can resolve most issues faster than a chargeback and avoid fees for both sides. If you file a chargeback, Dodo (as merchant of record) will handle the dispute with your card issuer; your license will be suspended pending resolution.

10. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights where you live. For example:

  • Colombia (Law 1480 — Consumer Statute): right of withdrawal and warranty protections where applicable to digital goods.
  • EU/UK: you may have a 14-day withdrawal right for distance purchases; our 14-day policy meets or exceeds that, and you consent to immediate performance at purchase (which ordinarily ends the withdrawal right) — we waive that technicality and honor the 14 days anyway.
  • Other jurisdictions: local consumer law applies where mandatory.

If a mandatory law gives you a longer window or different remedy, that law controls.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version with a new effective date. The policy in effect at your purchase date applies to that purchase.
