What we sell

Four steps, one deliverable: the number on your loops.

  1. Measure

    Per-turn wall, model span, pre-response span, unaccounted span, input tokens, cached tokens. You can run the first half of that yourself, on your own log, in the browser — nothing is uploaded.

    Open the free preview on your own log →

  2. Locate the tax

    Boot, pre-response, stream and tools per turn, measured from outside the harness; the token re-spend of carried context; the slow-turn tail; the share of turns that burn budget and produce nothing. Pre-response is reported as a bound — nothing outside the loop can split it into dispatch versus provider, so we do not pretend to.

  3. Cut it

    The one lever we measured as real and removable is boot: 929 ms of every turn if your loop spawns a process per turn, zero if it keeps one warm — 65 wall-hours a year for a 10-loop fleet. Everything else comes from your profile, not from a preset playbook.

  4. Lock it in

    A per-turn budget formula with your numbers, plus a regression check you keep and run after we leave: it fails at +20% wall or 5% waste, and it never re-baselines itself.

We retracted three of our own mechanisms. Cache discipline, startup-scan scoping and a transcript-size slope all failed to reproduce in our data (paper sections 6, 7 and 8), so we no longer sell them. Our cache sample did not separate fast turns from slow ones, and we publish that negative result instead of hiding it.

If the numbers do not survive your reading, tell us and we drop the pitch.
