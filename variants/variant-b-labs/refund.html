Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with every kit. If you're not, we'll make it right — quickly and without friction. This policy covers all digital goods sold via Dodo Payments on focalis.cc.

OUR PROMISE
14-day no-questions-asked refunds

Request a refund within 14 days of purchase for any reason — no justification needed. We process it within 48 hours via Dodo Payments.

1. Scope

This policy applies to all digital products sold by Focalis Labs LLC ("we," "us") through Dodo Payments as merchant of record, including HyperCapture, Signal Grid, Voice Forge, Frame Engine, Prompt Forge, Data Lens, the 3-Kit Stack, and the Builder Bundle. All products are digital goods delivered electronically (license keys + downloads); no physical shipment is involved.

2. 14-day refund window

  • Window: 14 calendar days from the date of purchase (the timestamp on your Dodo Payments receipt).
  • Reason: Any reason — the kit isn't a fit, you bought the wrong kit, or you changed your mind.
  • How to request: Email support@focalis.cc with your Dodo order ID (format: DODO-XXXX-XXXX) and the email used at checkout. You can also reply directly to your purchase receipt.
  • No restocking or penalty fees.

3. How refunds are processed

  1. Review: We confirm order ID and purchase date (usually within 24 hours on business days).
  2. Approval: If within the 14-day window, we approve and initiate the refund via Dodo Payments within 48 hours.
  3. Payout: Dodo Payments refunds to the original payment method. Banks/card processors typically post the credit in 5–10 business days after initiation. Timing after initiation is outside our control.
  4. License revocation: Upon refund, your license key and download access for the refunded Kit(s) are revoked. You agree to delete local copies. For Bundle refunds, all Kit licenses in the Bundle are revoked.

You will receive a refund confirmation email from Dodo Payments when the refund is issued. If you don't see it within 48 hours of our approval, contact support@focalis.cc.

4. After 14 days

After 14 days, purchases are considered final because digital goods cannot be "returned" in the traditional sense. However, we still want to help:

  • If a Kit is defective or materially not as described, contact us — we will either fix it, replace it, or issue a refund at our discretion even after 14 days.
  • If you experienced a technical delivery failure (no email, broken download), we will resolve delivery first and, if we cannot, refund the affected Kit regardless of window.
  • Discretionary refunds after 14 days are granted case-by-case. Email support@focalis.cc with details.

5. Bundle & multi-kit purchases

  • Builder Bundle ($499): Refundable in full within 14 days as a single purchase. Partial refunds for individual Kits within a Bundle are not available — the Bundle is one product.
  • 3-Kit Stack: Refundable as a whole within 14 days. If you want to swap a Kit (e.g., exchange Signal Grid for Data Lens), contact us within 14 days — we can handle swaps without a full refund where possible.

6. Non-refundable situations

We cannot issue refunds when:

  • The request is made more than 14 days after purchase (except for defects/delivery failures as above).
  • The purchase was made through an unauthorized reseller (all official sales are via Dodo Payments on focalis.cc).
  • There is evidence of license abuse (e.g., public redistribution, chargeback fraud). In that case we may revoke the license without refund, per our Terms.

Chargebacks: if you file a chargeback instead of contacting us, the payment processor will handle the dispute and your license will be suspended pending resolution. We strongly recommend emailing support@focalis.cc first — we resolve refund requests faster than a chargeback process.

7. Taxes & fees

VAT/GST/sales tax collected at checkout by Dodo Payments is refunded together with the purchase price when a refund is issued. Dodo handles tax reporting adjustments. Any payment-processor or currency-conversion fees charged by your bank are outside our control and may not be refundable.

9. Consumer rights

This policy does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer under applicable law (e.g., EEA/UK withdrawal rights or implied warranties). Where local law provides a longer withdrawal period or additional remedies, those rights remain available.

EEA/UK note: digital goods delivered immediately with your prior consent typically involve waiver of the 14-day withdrawal right once delivery begins; our voluntary 14-day refund policy provides equivalent or better protection regardless.
