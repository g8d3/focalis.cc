Privacy Policy

Focalis Labs LLC ("Focalis Labs," "we," "us") operates focalis.cc. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information — in plain language, because you deserve clarity.

1. Who we are

Focalis Labs LLC is a Delaware limited liability company headquartered at 8 The Green STE B, Dover, DE 19901, USA. Contact: hello@focalis.cc / support@focalis.cc. For data protection inquiries, contact hello@focalis.cc — we respond within 5 business days.

Payments on focalis.cc are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. Dodo handles checkout, payment processing, tax/VAT collection, invoicing, and delivery confirmations. Dodo's privacy practices apply to checkout data; see Dodo Payments Privacy Policy.

2. What we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact form: name, email, message content, and optional order ID when you write to us.
  • Purchase via Dodo: we receive order confirmations containing your email, product(s) purchased, amount, and license key. We do not receive or store your full payment card, bank, or PayPal credentials — those go directly to Dodo Payments and its payment processors.
  • Newsletter (if you opt in): email address only.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic analytics (page views, referrer, device type) via privacy-respecting analytics if enabled. We do not use cross-site tracking or fingerprinting.
  • Server logs (IP address, timestamps) for security and debugging, retained for 30 days.

What we don't collect

We don't collect sensitive personal data (health, biometrics, etc.), we don't run ads that track you across the web, and we don't sell your data — ever.

3. How we use your information

  • To deliver your purchase (license keys, download links, docs) and verify entitlement.
  • To respond to support, refund, and sales inquiries.
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, updates, security notices).
  • To send product updates only if you explicitly opt in — unsubscribe anytime.
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud, and comply with law.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

If you are in the EEA/UK, we process personal data under: contract (to fulfill your purchase), legitimate interests (support, security, product improvement), consent (marketing — withdraw anytime), and legal obligation (tax/invoice records).

5. Sharing & processors

We share data only with services needed to operate the site:

  • Dodo Payments — checkout, billing, tax, delivery. Acts as independent controller for payment data.
  • Email delivery (e.g., transactional email provider) — to send receipts and support replies.
  • Hosting & analytics — infrastructure providers (e.g., Vercel/Cloudflare-style) under data-processing agreements.

We require all processors to protect your data and use it only for our instructions. We never sell or rent personal data.

6. International transfers

We are based in the US. If you are outside the US, your data may be processed in the US and other countries where our processors operate. Where required, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

7. Retention

  • Order records: retained 7 years for tax/accounting obligations.
  • Support correspondence: retained 2 years, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Marketing opt-ins: until you unsubscribe.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. To exercise any right, email hello@focalis.cc. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

For California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA and we do not use sensitive personal information beyond permitted purposes.

9. Cookies

We use only essential cookies (e.g., to remember session and preferences). No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we add analytics cookies in the future, we will request consent where required and update this policy.

10. Data security

We use HTTPS, encryption at rest for order data, access controls, and least-privilege practices. No system is 100% secure, but we treat your data as we treat our own. If we detect a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and authorities as required by law.

11. Children

Our products are not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us for deletion.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you by email or site notice before the changes take effect.
