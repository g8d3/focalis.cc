Company — Focalis S.A.S. · Bogotá, Colombia

A small studio that measures
what AI agents actually cost.

Focalis S.A.S. is a software studio in Bogotá, Colombia (UTC-5), founded in 2019. We build measurement tools for AI agent fleets, and we sell one fixed-fee service on top of them: an audit that shows where the wall-clock and token time in your loops is actually going. Everything we claim is measured, and what we could not reproduce we publish as retracted.

What we sell on this site

The Agent Fleet Tax Audit — fixed fee, prepaid 50% and balance on delivery of the report. No success fee, no token-based billing, and we never ask for your API keys or funds.

  • ›Tier 1 — Single-Loop Measure, $290. One loop, 50+ measured turns, one breakdown page, 30-min readout.
  • ›Tier 2 — Fleet Audit, $1,400. Up to 5 loops, cache reported, transcript-size curve, prioritized cut list, regression gate you keep.
  • ›Tier 3 — Fleet Audit + Cut, $3,900. Fleet-wide, plus implementation support on the top cuts.
  • ›Fleet Tax Monitoring, $490/month. The instrument stays installed and we watch the trend after the cuts.

What is free

The instrument itself is open and free: pi-web-tps-monitor (MIT) records every turn of an agent session — wall, model span, the wait before the first token, tools, tokens and rate. The paper and the raw data are public at focalis.cc/tps, and the self-serve preview turns your own turn log into the same breakdown in the browser — nothing is uploaded.

Who delivers it

The studio is small on purpose. The audit is delivered by the engineer who built the instrument — there is no account team, no reseller and no outsourced analyst in the middle, which is also why the scope is fixed and the numbers are reproducible from the logs we hand back.
