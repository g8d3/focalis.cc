Story — Bogotá · 2019 → Now

We build because
we have to — and because we love to.

This is not a manifesto. It's a note. Focalis started without a business plan — just curiosity, a laptop, and the feeling that technology, done quietly and well, can give a little life back.

The dual relationship

From childhood I liked taking things apart. It felt like destiny more than decision — something that chose me. That love never left, but it became two-sided: technology excites me and at the same time it consumes me. There are weeks where I think: I should live more lightly.

Perhaps the obsession with efficiency comes from there — from early experiences where time and resources were scarce and every minute had to count. It's not always healthy, but it taught me to make systems that don't need constant attention.

The dream that keeps returning is simple: build something that works, hand it over, and not have to watch it again. Not abandonment — reliability. A product that solves a problem and stays solved, so I — and the people who use it — can spend time on other things. Like helping animals. Like noticing the world outside the screen.

The world we see

I live in a developing country. In the same block you can see precarity and privilege, often in the same house. The street teaches you quickly that the world is not cruel in a simple way — it's a spectrum between suffering and its opposite, with every shade in between, and they coexist at a few meters distance.

That taught me not to build for a fantasy user. We build for people who have limited time, limited budget, and real problems. If our software saves an hour a week, that's an hour that can go to care — for a family, for a dog, for rest.

"We don't want to present a perfect world. We want to present a world as it is — complex, unequal, beautiful — and show that small, reliable tools can make it a little more livable."

What Focalis is

Focalis S.A.S. — a small studio in Bogotá, Colombia. Founded as a freelance practice in 2019, incorporated shortly after. No venture capital, no growth-at-all-costs. Just continuous building.

Over the years that building produced an archive: experiments with AI, automation, browsers, video, trading, design systems. Internally we called them p1, p2, p3, p4 — convenient, but meaningless outside. Now we're slowly giving each one a proper name and deciding which deserve to become public works. The rest stay as lab notes.

Focalis is the house that holds that lab. The public site you're reading is the front door — calm, honest, with a few well-made pieces inside. Each product, when ready, lives on its own domain with its own documentation and billing. Focalis.cc doesn't need to sell everything — it just needs to tell the truth about how we build.

Principles

01 — Quiet

Make it work, then make it silent.

No notifications for the sake of engagement. No dashboard you must check. The best software is forgettable.

02 — Honest

No hype, no fake social proof.

We show what we've built, where it works and where it doesn't. No stock testimonials.

03 — Caring

Time saved is the metric.

If we save you an hour, that's the win. What you do with it — rest, create, help — is up to you. We also use part of our time and resources to help animals.
