Refund Policy

Focalis S.A.S., Bogotá, Colombia · hello@focalis.cc

We sell one service directly on this site: the Agent Fleet Tax Audit (focalis.cc/audit). Section 2 governs it. For anything bought on a product's own site, that site's refund terms apply and prevail over this summary.

1. Principle

We would rather refund than keep money for work we did not do or a promise we cannot show you. So refunds follow what has already been delivered, not a marketing rule. If we tell you the audit is not worth buying, there is nothing to refund — you never paid.

2. The Agent Fleet Tax Audit

  • Before we start. The fee is prepaid 50% to start. If you cancel before the instrument is installed and the measurement window opens, we refund that prepayment in full.
  • Once the window is running. The prepaid 50% covers measurement work already underway and is not refundable. The balance is only due on delivery of the report — we do not charge it early.
  • If we fail to deliver. Tier 1 and Tier 2: if we do not deliver the report and the agreed deliverables, everything you paid is refunded.
  • No lever found. If your numbers show no worthwhile cut, we tell you before the implementation half is due, and it is never charged.
  • Tier 3 implementation half. If the agreed target (20–50% off steady-state wall on the measured loops) is not met on the re-run protocol, the implementation half is refunded.
  • Fleet Tax Monitoring, $490/month. Cancel any month with 30 days' notice. A month in which monitoring already ran is not refundable; the following month is.

Where payment was collected by our merchant of record, it processes the refund back to the original payment method.

3. Other products

For products we operate on their own sites: 14 calendar days from purchase, if the software or service has not been substantially used (license not activated beyond a trial, or under 10% of the included quota). Ask within that window and we review it.

4. What isn't refundable

  • Work already delivered and signed off (a report you received, an implementation you reviewed)
  • Renewals that have already provided service in the period
  • Usage already consumed (API calls, runs, storage) beyond the thresholds above
  • Custom work that has started

5. How to request

Email hello@focalis.cc with subject "Refund — [tier or product]" and include your purchase email and date. We reply within 24h on business days and process approved refunds within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.

6. Questions?

Write to hello@focalis.cc or use the Contact page. See also the Terms of Service and Company page. We're a small team — we read every message.
