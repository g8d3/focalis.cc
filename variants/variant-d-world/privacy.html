Privacy Policy

Focalis S.A.S., Bogotá, Colombia · hello@focalis.cc

TL;DR: We collect the minimum needed to run the site and answer you. No tracking pixels, no selling data. Each product's own site has its own policy — this one covers focalis.cc.

1. Who we are

Controller: Focalis S.A.S., Bogotá, Colombia. Contact: hello@focalis.cc. For privacy requests, use the same address with subject "Privacy".

2. What we collect

  • Contact data you send via form or email (name, email, message).
  • Technical logs (IP, user agent) for security and debugging, kept briefly.
  • No analytics by default on focalis.cc. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, we'll update this notice and keep it anonymized.

4. The audit: your turn logs and your payment

Turn logs. The Agent Fleet Tax Audit (focalis.cc/audit) is measured from per-turn telemetry that our own instrument writes inside your workspace (.pi-web/tps-turns.jsonl). That file stays yours and stays in your workspace: we read only the copy you hand us or the access you grant, and we ask you to redact anything sensitive before we read it. We never ask for API keys, wallet keys, credentials or funds, and we do not need your prompts — the rows we use are timings, token counts and outcome flags. Reports we deliver contain aggregate figures and the turn rows you approved; we keep them for the engagement plus up to 12 months, then delete them on request.

Payment. When you pay by card, the card details are collected by the merchant of record (Dodo Payments), not by us. We receive only the billing name, email and transaction reference we need to invoice you and answer support questions.

5. Why we use it

To reply to you, to keep the site secure, and to comply with law. Legal basis (Colombia Law 1581 + GDPR where applicable): consent and legitimate interest.

6. Sharing

We don't sell your data. We share only with service providers needed to operate the site (hosting, email delivery), under confidentiality. Each product you visit from here is a separate controller with its own policy.

7. Retention

Messages: until your request is resolved plus up to 12 months for reference. Logs: up to 30 days. You can ask us to delete earlier.

8. Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, objection. Write to hello@focalis.cc. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the relevant authority.

9. Security

HTTPS, encrypted storage, least-privilege access. No system is perfect — if we find a breach affecting you, we'll notify you.

10. International transfers

Data may be processed in Colombia and where our hosting operates (EU/US). We use appropriate safeguards when needed.

11. Changes

We'll update the date above and note material changes here. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
