Pricing & billing — details

Dodo Payments is the merchant of record. They calculate VAT/GST at checkout, issue a compliant invoice, and handle tax remittance. Your receipt and invoice arrive by email immediately.
You can upgrade or downgrade at any time — changes are prorated. Cancel from your billing portal (via Dodo Payments) and access continues until the end of the current period. See Refund Policy for the 14-day refund window on new licenses.
Overage is measured monthly and capped so a traffic spike does not produce an uncapped bill. It is invoiced by Dodo Payments the following month. Hard caps and alerts are configurable in your workspace.
