About Focalis Systems

We build infrastructure that stays up when it matters.

Focalis Systems is a B2B systems integrator and software vendor. We sell licensed software and managed services for data movement, workflow automation, and system integration — the unglamorous plumbing that decides whether a company can ship reliably.

We are Focalis Technologies S.A.S., registered in Paris, France. Our customers are engineering and operations teams at companies of 20 to 2,000 employees, primarily in Europe and North America. We are not a marketplace, not an agency selling hours — we ship software with a contract and stand behind it.

How we work

Principles that survive procurement and production.

Contracts over claims

We put commitments in the agreement — SLA, response times, data residency, retention — not just the marketing page. If it matters to procurement, it belongs in the contract.

Boring reliability

We optimize for predictable operation: versioned releases, changelogs, rollback, tested restores, and audit logs from day one. No surprise deprecations.

Your data, your boundary

EU primary residency, self-host on Enterprise, and a public sub-processor list. We minimize what we collect and document what we must process. See our Privacy Policy.
