1. Who we are and scope

Focalis Technologies S.A.S. is the data controller for personal data collected via our website, sales and support interactions, and account administration for Focalis products (Data Pipeline, Automation Suite, Integration Layer). Where we process customer data on your behalf as part of providing the software (for example, records you send through a pipeline or automation), we act as a processor under GDPR — governed by our DPA. This Policy describes our practices as controller; the DPA governs processor activity.

2. Personal data we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Contact and account information: name, work email, company, role, phone, billing address (where required for invoicing coordination), workspace configuration, and messages you send via forms, email, or calls.
  • Support and procurement data: support tickets, questionnaire responses, DPA execution details, and account administration logs (who configured what, when).
  • Payment data: We do not store full card numbers or bank details. Payments, VAT calculation, invoicing, and tax remittance are handled by Dodo Payments as merchant of record. We receive payment confirmations, invoice metadata, and limited billing contact information from Dodo Payments to fulfill your license and support obligations.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage and logs: When you use our website or licensed software (cloud-hosted option), we collect operational logs — IP address, user agent, pages or endpoints accessed, timestamps, error and performance metrics — for security, reliability, and support.
  • Cookies and similar: This static site variant uses no advertising trackers. If we deploy analytics or preference cookies in other variants or deployments, we present a consent banner where required. Essential cookies needed for security and functionality do not require consent.

2.3 Data we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (health, biometrics, political opinions, etc.) and you should not send such data through our products unless your use case requires it and you have a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards. Customer content sent through pipelines or automations is your data — you control it.

Where GDPR applies, we process personal data on the bases listed above: performance of a contract, legitimate interests (balanced against your rights — e.g., operating a secure service, communicating about it, and improving it), compliance with legal obligations, and consent where we have requested it (for example, optional marketing). You may withdraw consent at any time where it is the basis for processing — withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We share personal data only as needed to operate our business and as described here:

  • Sub-processors listed below, under contract and only for the stated purposes.
  • Dodo Payments as merchant of record for checkout, invoicing, VAT, and tax handling.
  • Service providers under our control (processors) such as hosting, email delivery, error monitoring, and support tooling.
  • Legal and safety: where required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of our services.
  • Business transfer: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice to customers and continued protection of data.

We require processors to handle data only on our instructions, with confidentiality, security, and data-protection commitments, and to assist us with data-subject requests and breach notification.

7. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

For customers who handle personal data through our products, we offer a DPA that incorporates:

  • The EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for international transfers where applicable, with appropriate supplementary measures;
  • A description of processing (subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, categories of data and data subjects);
  • Our commitments on sub-processors, confidentiality, security, breach notification, audit assistance, and return/deletion at termination;
  • Your instructions as controller and our obligation to act only on them (or where required by law, with notice).

To execute the DPA, contact legal@focalis.cc or your account contact. Execution does not delay your ability to use the product — the DPA can be countersigned after purchase.

8. International transfers

We primarily process data in the EU (Frankfurt). Where you select US residency or where a processor operates globally (e.g., Dodo Payments as merchant of record), transfers outside the EEA/UK are protected by SCCs, adequacy decisions where available, or other lawful mechanisms, plus technical measures (encryption in transit and at rest) and contractual commitments. We document transfer mechanisms in the DPA.

9. Retention

  • Account and billing records: retained for the life of the account and for 6 years after closure (or longer where tax or accounting law requires).
  • Support and procurement correspondence: up to 3 years after last interaction, or longer if needed for dispute resolution.
  • Operational logs: 12 months for audit and security, then aggregated or deleted; backups are retained 30 days with point-in-time recovery.
  • Customer content (processor role): retained per your configuration and contract; upon termination we return or delete per DPA terms (generally within 30 days, with backup expiry within 90 days).

We apply minimization — we keep what is needed for the purpose and delete or anonymize the rest.

10. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures including: encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), access control with least privilege and MFA for internal access, audit logging, daily backups with tested restores, network isolation, and incident response procedures. We maintain a security overview and respond to questionnaires within one business day — contact legal@focalis.cc. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat security as a contractual commitment, not a slogan.

11. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, update, or delete your personal data;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, or request data portability;
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing;
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g., CNIL in France, or your local authority).

To exercise rights, contact legal@focalis.cc or contact@focalis.cc. We respond within 30 days (sooner where law requires). We may need to verify identity and to preserve data where law or the contract requires. Where we act as processor, we assist the controller in fulfilling requests — please direct subject requests to the controller first.

12. Children

Our products and site are for business use and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this Policy
